# Monitoring the class's progress

## Before looking at Moodle

If `knowledge/progress.md` exists, read it first: it's your own history of earlier
reviews — class-level, without names — so you can compare ("3 students without the first
practice last week, 2 now") and focus on what's changed. Who exactly is behind you read live
from Moodle each time; it goes in your reply to the teacher, never in the knowledge base.

## What to check

Review the course's progress/grades report (teacher view, with every student) and split
what you find into two groups:

- **Students falling behind**: haven't started activities whose deadline has passed or
  is close, or have a grade clearly below average with no apparent justification (e.g.
  an empty submission).
- **The rest**: progressing normally, no need to dwell on each of them.

## How to prioritize

1. First, students with no activity submitted at all — it's the clearest sign something
   is wrong, more so than a low grade on a single activity.
2. Then, students with overdue, unsubmitted deadlines.
3. Last, patterns that repeat (several students failing the same thing) — that points to
   a problem with the course content itself, not a specific student, and is worth
   noting separately.

Keep observed data and inference separate in how you report this: "hasn't submitted
activity X" is something you read directly off the grades page; "the deadline was
probably too tight" or "this activity is confusing several students" is your own reading
of a pattern. State both, but don't present the second kind as if it were as certain as
the first.

## What to summarize when done

Don't list every student's grade one by one — summarize: how many are falling behind and
why (no submission / overdue deadline / low grade), and whether there's a pattern
suggesting a specific activity or topic in the course needs review.

## If it's serious or widespread

If several students are falling behind for the same reason (a deadline, a confusing
activity) and you're in a context where you can publish (`run` in `guided` mode, or
`chat`), consider posting an announcement reminding them of the deadline or clarifying
the activity, instead of just noting it — see the announcements section of the
`forum` skill. A single student falling behind on their own doesn't justify it; a
pattern affecting several does.

## Update knowledge/progress.md

Add an entry with this review's date and the summary above, as counts and patterns
without any student's name — don't rewrite earlier entries, it's a history. The knowledge base is the agent's memory across sessions: without
this, every progress review would start from zero and you wouldn't be able to tell
whether the class is catching up or falling further behind.
