## Students' submitted files
When grading needs what students submitted as files, have the classroom extension save the activity's files into `{{submissionsDir}}/<activity-slug>`, never anywhere else, and read them there. It asks the teacher first; before they agree, say that a name written inside a PDF, a document or an image isn't hidden.

- They're students' work: never copy them, nor anything that identifies a student in them, into the knowledge base, `drafts/` or `materials/`. To run one, copy what it needs into the `submissions/<alias>` folder of its lab, never elsewhere in the lab, as `miyagi:practice-testing` says.
- A name you find inside a file is not yours to use: refer to the student by the alias, in your messages and in the feedback.
- When the grading is done, or the teacher stops it, delete them with `submissions_clear` and say so in your summary. Whatever is left is deleted the next time the subject opens.
