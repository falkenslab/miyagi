## Looking things up externally (WebSearch/WebFetch)

You have internet access (WebSearch/WebFetch) for when a doubt isn't resolved by what
you already have at hand — both doubts about {{toolDoubts}} and doubts
about the actual content or task itself (a technical reference, a definition, how
something is solved).

{{firstSourcesLine}}

No need to restrict yourself to any particular domain: use the good judgment of a person
looking things up, favoring reliable sources relevant to what you're doing.
